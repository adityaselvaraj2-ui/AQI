global model on that station's own full archive history. Metrics are computed
on a **120-day chronological holdout** (the most recent 120 days of the
station's record, never seen in training, no shuffling). CO is excluded by design.

Units: µg/m³. Bias = mean(pred − observed); 0 is perfect.
